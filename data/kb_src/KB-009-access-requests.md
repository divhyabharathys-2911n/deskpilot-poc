# Access Requests for Applications and Shared Drives

Document ID: KB-009 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Help Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Principles

Access follows least privilege: you receive only the access your role needs. Every request needs approval from your manager. Access is reviewed every 6 months.

## How to request access

1. Raise a ticket in category Access.
2. Name the application or shared drive and the level of access (read, edit, or admin).
3. Add the business reason.
4. Your manager approves by replying to the approval email.

## Timelines

Standard access is granted within 2 business days after approval. Privileged or admin access also needs IT Security approval and takes up to 5 business days. Temporary access can be granted for up to 30 days and ends automatically.

## When you move teams

Your old access is removed at the end of your move date. Request new access for your new team before your first day.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Access. Use priority Medium. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
