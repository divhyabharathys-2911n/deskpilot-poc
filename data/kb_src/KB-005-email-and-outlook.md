# Email and Outlook

Document ID: KB-005 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Help Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Mailbox limits

Each mailbox has 50 GB of storage and a 100 GB online archive. The maximum message size, including attachments, is 25 MB. For larger files, share a link from the company file share.

## Common problems and fixes

1. Outlook shows "Disconnected". Check your internet connection, then close and reopen Outlook. If the problem continues, restart the computer.
2. Outlook is stuck on "Loading profile". Open Control Panel, open Mail, choose Show Profiles, add a new profile, and set it as the default.
3. A deleted message is needed. Open Deleted Items, then choose Recover items deleted from this folder. Deleted items can be recovered for 30 days.
4. Shared mailbox access. Submit an access request. The mailbox owner and your manager must approve.
5. Set an out-of-office reply in Outlook under Automatic Replies.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Email. Use priority Medium. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
