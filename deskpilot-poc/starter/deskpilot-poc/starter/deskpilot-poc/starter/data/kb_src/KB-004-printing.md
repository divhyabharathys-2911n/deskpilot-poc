# Printing and Scanning

Document ID: KB-004 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Help Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## How printing works

Fabrikam uses Follow-Me printing. Send your job to the queue named FABRIKAM-FOLLOWME, then tap your badge on any office printer to release it. Jobs that are not released are deleted after 24 hours.

## Common problems and fixes

1. The job does not appear at the printer. Check that you sent it to FABRIKAM-FOLLOWME, not to a personal or local printer.
2. Paper jam. Open the side door, remove the jammed sheet in the direction of the paper path, and close the door.
3. Toner is low or empty. Facilities replaces toner within 1 business day. Do not open the toner compartment yourself.
4. Badge is not read. Hold the badge flat against the reader for two seconds. If it still fails, ask the help desk to re-register the badge.
5. Scan to email sends to your work email address only.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Printer. Use priority Low. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
