# Password Reset and Account Lockout

Document ID: KB-002 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Help Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Password policy

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords expire every 90 days. You cannot reuse any of your last 12 passwords. An account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. The lock clears by itself after 15 minutes.

## Reset your own password

1. Go to https://passwordreset.fabrikam.example.
2. Enter your work email address.
3. Approve the sign-in on your Fabrikam Authenticator app.
4. Choose a new password that follows the policy above.

## If you cannot use self-service

If you have lost access to your authenticator app, the help desk must verify your identity. Your manager must confirm the request by email. The help desk then issues a temporary password that must be changed at the next sign-in.

## Security reminders

The help desk never asks for your password. Never share your password in chat, email, or tickets.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Password. Use priority Medium. Use priority High if you cannot sign in at all and have a deadline today. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
