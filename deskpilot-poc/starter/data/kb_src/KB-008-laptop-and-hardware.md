# Laptop and Hardware Issues

Document ID: KB-008 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Help Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## Laptop lifecycle

Fabrikam replaces laptops every 4 years. If your laptop cannot be repaired the same day, the help desk can issue a loaner laptop within 1 business day.

## Common problems and fixes

1. Slow laptop. Restart at least once a week and keep at least 10 percent of the disk free. Close apps you are not using.
2. Screen flickers. Update the display driver in Software Center, then restart. Try an external monitor to check whether the screen or the graphics card is the cause.
3. Battery drains fast. Open Settings, then Power, and check which apps use the most battery.
4. Docking station not detected. Unplug the dock for 30 seconds, plug it back in, and restart the laptop.

## Safety warning: swollen battery

If the laptop case bulges or the trackpad lifts, stop using the laptop. Unplug it, do not charge it, and keep it away from other items. Raise a Critical ticket in category Laptop and bring the laptop to the IT desk.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Laptop. Use priority Medium. Use priority High if you cannot work. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
