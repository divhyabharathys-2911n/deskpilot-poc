# Reporting Security Incidents and Phishing

Document ID: KB-010 | Owner: Fabrikam IT Security | Last reviewed: 2026-09-01

## What to report

Report suspicious emails, clicked phishing links, malware warnings, and lost or stolen laptops or phones. Report within 1 hour of noticing the problem.

## Phishing email

1. Do not click links or open attachments.
2. Use the Report Phishing button in Outlook.
3. Do not forward the email to anyone.

## You clicked a link or opened an attachment

1. Disconnect from Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable.
2. Do not turn off the laptop.
3. Raise a Critical ticket in category Security or call the IT Security line on extension 4000.
4. Change your password from another device after IT Security tells you it is safe.

## Lost or stolen device

Report it within 1 hour. IT Security locks the device remotely and removes company data.

## Response

IT Security responds to Critical tickets within 15 minutes. Do not delete evidence such as the suspicious email.

## If the steps fail

Raise a ticket in category Security with priority Critical. Response targets: Critical 1 hour, High 4 hours, Medium 1 business day, Low 3 business days.
